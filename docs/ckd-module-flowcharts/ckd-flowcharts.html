資料時效：current 是什麼意思

多個模組會問「時效是否為 current」。那不是「有沒有這筆資料」，而是採檢日期距今幾天， 對照該項目自己的建議回檢間隔 intervalDays：

距今天數狀態這些模組如何處理

≤ intervalDays × 1.5due一律視同沒有可用數值，走 needs-data
更久overdue
沒有日期missing

目前 CKD pack 只認 current，due 與 overdue 沒有分別對待。

ckd-classification

指引依據

KDIGO 2024 CKD CGA classification; Tables 1–3 PDF p.19 ↗

第 1 章 → CKD 定義與 C/G/A 分類

CKD 依病因（C）、GFR 類別（G1–G5）與白蛋白尿類別（A1–A3）分類；G/A 分期需使用可判讀的 eGFR 與定量 ACR。

CGA classification

CKD is classified based on Cause, GFR category (G1–G5), and Albuminuria category (A1–A3), abbreviated as CGA.

KDIGO 2024 CKD Practice Points 1.1.3.1–1.1.3.2 PDF p.34 ↗

第 1 章 → CKD 慢性化證據

CKD 慢性化至少需 3 個月，可由既往檢驗、診斷、影像或重複量測建立；不可由單次異常推定。

Practice Point 1.1.3.1

Proof of chronicity (duration of a minimum of 3 months) can be established by: (i) review of past measurements/estimations of GFR; (ii) review of past measurements of albuminuria or proteinuria and urine microscopic examinations; (iii) imaging findings such as reduced kidney size and reduction in cortical thickness; (iv) kidney pathological findings such as fibrosis and atrophy; (v) medical history, especially conditions known to cause or contribute to CKD; (vi) repeat measurements within and beyond the 3-month point.

Practice Point 1.1.3.2

Do not assume chronicity based upon a single abnormal level for eGFR and ACR, as the finding could be the result of a recent acute kidney injury (AKI) event or acute kidney disease (AKD).

02

腎衰竭風險（KFRE）

ckd-kidney-failure-risk

四變數 KFRE，非北美校正。與醫學計算機分頁共用同一份實作，同一病人不會出現兩個數字。

模組閘門：G 分期不存在、或為 G1／G2 時整張卡不產生——風險方程式未在 G1–G2 驗證。
  • 輸入：年齡 · 性別 · eGFR · UACR
    • 定量且時效為 current？（依 G/A 分期，91–365 天）
      • 以定量值代入
      • 以明確下限值代入，標示為下限情境
      • 無法計算
  • 四項輸入齊全且 UACR > 0？
    • needs-data · medium查找或補做必要輸入——缺輸入是資料落差，不是急症
    • calculateKfre() → 2 年／5 年絕對風險
      • needs-data方向性保守情境，不視為正式估計；priority 一律 medium——判不出來的問題沒有傷害時鐘，該做的是補定量 UACR
      • actionable · highKRT 準備／多專業照護時機
      • review · medium可輔助決定腎臟專科轉介
      • no-action · routine仍以主要卡片保留，供醫師看到並記錄結果

指引依據

KDIGO 2024 CKD Recommendation 2.2.1; Practice Points 2.2.1-2.2.4 1A PDF p.41 ↗

第 2 章 → 腎衰竭風險預測

CKD G3-G5 應使用外部驗證的風險方程式；5 年風險 3%-5% 可輔助轉介，2 年風險 >10% 與 >40% 可輔助多專業照護及腎臟替代治療準備時機。

Recommendation 2.2.1

In people with CKD G3–G5, we recommend using an externally validated risk equation to estimate the absolute risk of kidney failure (1A).

Practice Points 2.2.1–2.2.4

A 5-year kidney failure risk of 3%–5% can be used to determine need for nephrology referral in addition to criteria based on eGFR or urine ACR, and other clinical considerations. A 2-year kidney failure risk of >10% can be used to determine the timing of multidisciplinary care in addition to eGFR-based criteria and other clinical considerations. A 2-year kidney failure risk threshold of >40% can be used to determine the modality education, timing of preparation for kidney replacement therapy (KRT) including vascular access planning or referral for transplantation, in addition to eGFR-based criteria and other clinical considerations. Note that risk prediction equations developed for use in people with CKD G3–G5, may not be valid for use in those with CKD G1–G2.

03

ACEI／ARB 腎臟保護

ckd-rasi-strategy

適應症由白蛋白尿分級決定，不由 CKD 分期決定。

  • 已有 ACEI／ARB 處方？
    • review · medium指引要求最大耐受劑量（Rec 3.6.1），核對劑量與耐受性
    • no-action · routine「未找到」不等於病人未服用，反之亦然
    • 有定量 UACR？
      • needs-data · medium補定量 UACR 才能判定適應症
      • A 分期為 A2 或 A3？
        • no-action · routine目前無白蛋白尿適應症
        • actionable · medium建議評估開始
        • review · medium先核對暫停原因與實際使用

指引依據

KDIGO 2024 CKD Recommendations 3.6.1–3.6.4; Practice Points 3.6.1, 3.6.2, 3.6.4, 3.6.7 1B / 2C PDF p.43 ↗

第 3 章 → RAS 抑制劑

依糖尿病與 A2/A3 白蛋白尿評估 ACEI 或 ARB；開始或增量後 2–4 週監測，4 週內 creatinine 上升 >30% 才觸發原因評估，eGFR <30 本身不是停藥理由。

Recommendation 3.6.1

We recommend starting renin-angiotensin-system inhibitors (RASi) (angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor [ACEi] or angiotensin II receptor blocker [ARB]) for people with CKD and severely increased albuminuria (G1–G4, A3) without diabetes (1B).

Recommendation 3.6.2

We suggest starting RASi (ACEi or ARB) for people with CKD and moderately increased albuminuria (G1–G4, A2) without diabetes (2C).

Recommendation 3.6.3

We recommend starting RASi (ACEi or ARB) for people with CKD and moderately-to-severely increased albuminuria (G1–G4, A2 and A3) with diabetes (1B).

Recommendation 3.6.4

We recommend avoiding any combination of ACEi, ARB, and direct renin inhibitor (DRI) therapy in people with CKD, with or without diabetes (1B).

Practice Point 3.6.1

RASi (ACEi or ARB) should be administered using the highest approved dose that is tolerated to achieve the benefits described because the proven benefits were achieved in trials using these doses.

Practice Point 3.6.2

Changes in BP, serum creatinine, and serum potassium should be checked within 2–4 weeks of initiation or increase in the dose of a RASi, depending on the current GFR and serum potassium.

Practice Point 3.6.4

Continue ACEi or ARB therapy unless serum creatinine rises by more than 30% within 4 weeks following initiation of treatment or an increase in dose.

Practice Point 3.6.7

Continue ACEi or ARB in people with CKD even when the eGFR falls below 30 ml/min per 1.73 m2.

04

SGLT2i 心腎保護

ckd-sglt2-strategy

三條適應症路徑任一成立即為候選；eGFR 下限一律 20。

  • 已有 SGLT2i 處方？
    • no-action · routine
    • 候選條件：eGFR ≥ 20 且下列任一成立？
      • 心衰竭診斷
      • UACR ≥ 200 mg/g
      • eGFR ≤ 45
      • actionable · medium建議評估使用
      • review · medium
      • eGFR 缺值，或 eGFR > 45 且無心衰竭且無 UACR？
        • needs-data · medium
        • no-action · routine

指引依據

KDIGO 2024 CKD Recommendations 3.7.2–3.7.3 1A / 2B PDF p.44 ↗

第 3 章 → SGLT2 抑制劑

成人 CKD 在 eGFR ≥20 且 ACR ≥200 mg/g 或合併心衰竭時建議 SGLT2 抑制劑；eGFR 20–45 且 ACR <200 mg/g 時亦可評估。

Recommendation 3.7.2

We recommend treating adults with CKD with an SGLT2i for the following (1A): eGFR ≥20 ml/min per 1.73 m2 with urine ACR ≥200 mg/g (≥20 mg/mmol), or heart failure, irrespective of level of albuminuria.

Recommendation 3.7.3

We suggest treating adults with eGFR 20 to 45 ml/min per 1.73 m2 with urine ACR <200 mg/g (<20 mg/mmol) with an SGLT2i (2B).

Finerenone 腎臟保護

ckd-finerenone-strategy

條件最多的一張。K 值是硬性前置，白蛋白尿必須是「持續」而非單次。

模組閘門：eligibleDiseasePackIds 不含 dm-poc 時整張卡不產生——適應症是糖尿病腎病變，非糖尿病 CKD 病人看不到這張卡。
  • K > 5.0 mmol/L？
    • review不應開始；先處理高血鉀並複查（此判斷優先於「已有處方」）
    • 已有 finerenone 處方？
      • no-action
      • 候選條件：eGFR > 25 且 UACR > 30？
        • review先核對暫停原因
        • needs-data
        • needs-data需 ≥ 2 個不同日期的定量 UACR > 30；單次不足以開藥
        • review例如 RASi 是否為最大耐受劑量

        • no-action

第 3 章 → 非類固醇 MRA

T2D 合併 CKD 只有在 eGFR >25、正常血鉀、持續 UACR >30 mg/g 且已使用最大耐受 RASi 後，才進一步評估具心腎效益的非類固醇 MRA。

We suggest a nonsteroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist with proven kidney or cardiovascular benefit for adults with T2D, an eGFR >25 ml/min per 1.73 m2, normal serum potassium concentration, and albuminuria (>30 mg/g [>3 mg/mmol]) despite maximum tolerated dose of RAS inhibitor (RASi) (2A).

06

Statin 心血管保護

ckd-cardiovascular-risk

CKD 本身即為心血管高風險。指引本身就不設 LDL 起始門檻——三條建議只看年齡、eGFR 與共病。這張卡不看 LDL 不是省略，是照指引。

  • 已有 statin 處方？
    • no-action · routine
    • 應評估？年齡 ≥ 50 或 糖尿病 或 ASCVD
      • review · medium
      • actionable · medium建議評估使用 statin
      • needs-data · medium
      • no-action · routine
為什麼不看 LDL——這是指引刻意的：KDIGO 在第 3.15.1 節寫明對 CKD 採 fire-and-forget，理由是「起始依風險決定，而療效與膽固醇高低無關」，並說多數人不需要追蹤血脂來調藥。所以 LDL 50 與 LDL 158 只要落在同一風險族群，判定完全一樣。 整章唯一的膽固醇數字用途不同：TC > 7.5 mmol/L（290 mg/dL）合併早發性缺血性心臟病的個人或一等親病史時，指引要的是懷疑家族性高膽固醇血症並轉介專科——那是找病因，不是 statin 的起始門檻。本卡未做此判定，家族史不在資料範圍。 高齡與衰弱：年齡 ≥ 90、frail 或 very-complex-poor-health 不改變判定，只在文案加上個人化與去處方的提醒。三者之中目前只有年齡會成立——衰弱與健康狀態分層需要正式評估，健康存摺與門診資料都沒有，契約欄位留著等未來的資料來源。

指引依據

KDIGO 2024 CKD Recommendations 3.15.1.1–3.15.1.3; Practice Point 3.15.1.2 1A / 1B / 2A PDF p.47 ↗

第 3 章 → CKD 血脂管理

成人 CKD 的 statin 依年齡與 eGFR 分層：≥50 歲且 eGFR <60（G3a–G5、未透析未移植）建議 statin 或 statin/ezetimibe（1A）；≥50 歲且 eGFR ≥60 建議 statin（1B）；18–49 歲需有冠心病、糖尿病、既往中風或十年冠心病風險 >10% 之一（2A）。三條都不含 LDL 門檻。

第 3.15.1 節內文（p. 119）

In adults with newly identified CKD, it has been recommended to evaluate their lipid profile (total cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, and triglycerides), but follow-up lipid measurements are not required for the majority of people (i.e., a fire-and-forget policy is recommended). This is because treatment initiation is based on risk, and the benefits of statin-based therapy have been shown to be independent of the level of cholesterol.

Recommendations 3.15.1.1–3.15.1.3

In adults aged ≥50 years with eGFR <60 ml/min per 1.73 m2 but not treated with chronic dialysis or kidney transplantation (GFR categories G3a–G5), we recommend treatment with a statin or statin/ezetimibe combination (1A). In adults aged ≥50 years with CKD and eGFR ≥60 ml/min per 1.73 m2 (GFR categories G1–G2), we recommend treatment with a statin (1B). In adults aged 18–49 years with CKD but not treated with chronic dialysis or kidney transplantation, we suggest statin treatment in people with one or more of the following (2A): known coronary disease (myocardial infarction or coronary revascularization), diabetes mellitus, prior ischemic stroke, or estimated 10-year incidence of coronary death or nonfatal myocardial infarction >10%.

Practice Point 3.15.1.2（p. 119）

In people with CKD, choose statin-based regimens to maximize the absolute reduction in low-density lipoprotein (LDL) cholesterol to achieve the largest treatment benefits.

07

腎功能趨勢

ckd-monitoring

比較最近兩筆可用 eGFR 的百分比變化，門檻取自 KDIGO 的「超出預期變異」。

  • 趨勢來源至少兩筆數值？
    • needs-data · medium無法建立可比較趨勢
    • 取最近兩筆　Δ% = (新 − 舊) ÷ 舊 × 100
      • review · medium需評估變化原因
      • needs-data · medium
      • no-action · routine

指引依據

KDIGO 2024 CKD Practice Points 2.1.1-2.1.5 PDF p.40 ↗

第 2 章 → GFR 與 ACR 追蹤

CKD 至少每年評估 eGFR 與白蛋白尿；高風險者增加頻率。後續 eGFR 變化超過 20% 或 ACR 倍增需評估。

Practice Point 2.1.1

Assess albuminuria in adults, or albuminuria/proteinuria in children, and GFR at least annually in people with CKD.

Practice Point 2.1.2

Assess albuminuria and GFR more often for individuals at higher risk of CKD progression when measurement will impact therapeutic decisions.

Practice Point 2.1.3

For people with CKD, a change in eGFR of >20% on a subsequent test exceeds the expected variability and warrants evaluation.

Practice Point 2.1.4

Among people with CKD who initiate hemodynamically active therapies, GFR reductions of >30% on subsequent testing exceed the expected variability and warrant evaluation.

Practice Point 2.1.5

For albuminuria monitoring of people with CKD, a doubling of the ACR on a subsequent test exceeds laboratory variability and warrants evaluation.

血壓與體液狀態

ckd-blood-pressure-volume

過期血壓不參與判定；高齡或衰弱情境不自動套用強化目標——但實際上只有年齡 ≥ 90 會成立，衰弱分層目前沒有資料來源。

  • 血壓時效為 current？（採檢距今 ≤ 180 天）

腎毒性與腎功能劑量

兩級的理由：NSAID 在進展期 CKD 可在數日內誘發 AKI，屬 high；其他腎毒性藥物多半給藥當下已在監測或以月為尺度作用，放 medium 才不會讓 high 失去意義。沒掃到時不列藥名：卡片只顯示掃描範圍的筆數（例如「目前用藥 28 筆，已全數掃描」），藥名只在掃到時列出——在一張標題寫腎毒性的卡上列出未命中的藥名，會被讀成那些就是掃描結果。筆數保留是因為它是分母：少了它，「無需處理」分不出是掃了 28 筆都乾淨，還是根本沒有用藥資料可掃。完整清單見左側「用藥」，卡片上的資料來源也會直接跳到那裡。 掃描只看得到健保處方，成藥與中草藥大多不在裡面：「沒掃到」不等於沒有，「掃到」也不等於正在使用。

Practice Point 4.2.2

Practice Point 4.2.3

Recommendation 3.2.2 (2D, p. 52)

In people with CKD not receiving dialysis, including kidney transplant recipients and children, the selection of Hb concentration at which ESA therapy is initiated should consider the presence of symptoms attributable to anemia, the potential benefits of higher Hb concentration, and the potential harms of RBC transfusions or ESA therapy (2D).

為什麼沒有門檻：KDIGO 2017 把 2009 版的「維持在正常範圍」全部拿掉了——磷只說把已升高的往正常範圍「降」（Rec 4.1.2，不給目標值），鈣只說「避免高血鈣」（Rec 4.1.3），未透析 PTH 更直接寫「the optimal PTH level is not known」（Rec 4.2.1）。唯一的數字目標是透析病人的 iPTH 2–9 倍上限，而本卡不帶透析門檻。所以卡片給不出門檻，是指引本身沒有。
「進行性」的定義很嚴格：整段連續值每一步都同方向才算，只是比上次高不算。可矯正因素中的高血磷、低血鈣能從檢驗判定並直接列出；磷攝取過高與維生素 D 缺乏病歷給不了，一律列入待補而不推定。
含鈣製劑的判讀是有條件的：碳酸鈣沒有磷結合劑專屬 ATC（它同時是制酸劑與鈣補充劑），所以卡片說的是「若作為磷結合劑使用，指引建議限制用量」，不直接斷定用途。ATC 逐碼列舉：含鈣 V03AE04 · 07，非含鈣 V03AE02 · 03 · 05 · 06 · 08——V03AE 同時裝鉀結合劑（01 · 09 · 10），前綴會把不結合磷的藥算進來。

Recommendation 4.1.1

In patients with CKD G3a–G5D, treatments of CKD-MBD should be based on serial assessments of phosphate, calcium, and PTH levels, considered together (Not Graded).

第 4.2 節 → 未透析 CKD 的 PTH

未透析 CKD 的最佳 PTH 值未知；PTH 持續高於檢驗室正常上限或呈進行性上升時，評估可矯正因素。

Recommendation 4.2.1 (2C)

In patients with CKD G3a–G5 not on dialysis, the optimal PTH level is not known. However, we suggest that patients with levels of intact PTH progressively rising or persistently above the upper normal limit for the assay be evaluated for modifiable factors, including hyperphosphatemia, hypocalcemia, high phosphate intake, and vitamin D deficiency (2C).

ckd-nutrition

永遠不會給出處方的模組：飲食攝取、體重變化與肌少症都無法從檢驗推定，本卡只負責提醒去做評估。

模組閘門：G 分期非 G3a–G5 時整張卡不產生。

    • review · medium　高齡情境：先評估肌少症與衰弱再設定營養目標
    • no-action · routine　先完成飲食與營養風險評估

衰弱不作為分支：健康存摺與門診資料都沒有衰弱或功能評估，若照 KDIGO 的高齡條文拉出一支「已有衰弱評估」，那支永遠不會走到，而它的措辭本身就是病歷撐不起的宣稱。因此本卡不判衰弱，只把它列進待補欄位並提醒去做——年齡本身不等於衰弱，這句也寫在卡片理由裡。

第 5 章 → 轉介腎臟專科照護

轉介情境依 Figure 48 分四類：病因（原因不明、遺傳性腎病、反覆廣泛腎結石）、eGFR／腎衰竭風險（5 年 KRT 風險 >3–5%、eGFR <30、GFR 持續下降 >20%，或啟動影響腎血流治療後 >30%）、白蛋白尿與鏡下血尿（ACR ≥300 合併血尿、白蛋白尿倍增、ACR >700、紅血球圓柱體或 RBC >20/HPF），以及併發症（需四種以上降壓藥的難治型高血壓、持續血鉀異常、酸中毒、貧血、骨病變、營養不良）。

Practice Point 5.1.1

Refer adults with CKD to specialist kidney care services in the circumstances listed in Figure 48.

Figure 48 — eGFR/risk of KRT

A >3%–5% 5-year risk of requiring KRT measured using a validated risk equation; eGFR <30 ml/min per 1.73 m2; a sustained fall in GFR of >20% or >30% in those people initiating hemodynamically active therapies.

Figure 48 — Albuminuria and microscopic hematuria

Consistent finding of significant albuminuria (ACR ≥300 mg/g [≥30 mg/mmol] or AER ≥300 mg/24 hours, approximately equivalent to PCR ≥500 mg/g [≥50 mg/mmol] or PER ≥500 mg/24 h) in combination with hematuria; ≥2-fold increase in albuminuria in people with significant albuminuria undergoing monitoring; a consistent finding of ACR >700 mg/g [>70 mg/mmol]; urinary red cell casts, RBC >20 per high power field sustained and not readily explained.

Figure 48 — Others / Causes

CKD and hypertension refractory to treatment ≥4 antihypertensive agents; persistent abnormalities of serum potassium; acidosis; anemia; bone disease; malnutrition. — Cause of CKD is uncertain; hereditary kidney disease; recurrent extensive nephrolithiasis.
